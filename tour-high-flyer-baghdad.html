High Flyer
Baghdad & Babylon

🕒 4 days👥 Max 10🌍 Iraq🏛️ Mesopotamia essentials

Four days in the heart of ancient Mesopotamia — Baghdad and Babylon. This express tour focuses on Iraq's capital city and the most famous ancient site in the country. The National Museum of Iraq alone justifies the journey, and Babylon completes one of the most powerful cultural days available to any traveller.

This is the right choice for first-time visitors to Iraq who want to understand the historical depth of the country before committing to a longer tour, or for travellers passing through Baghdad with a few days to spare.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Baghdad International Airport

    Meet and greet at Baghdad airport. Transfer to hotel in central Baghdad. Evening briefing and walk along the Tigris Corniche.

  2. 2

    Baghdad — Museums, mosques and markets

    Iraq National Museum (the most significant collection of Mesopotamian antiquities in the world). Mutanabbi Street book market. Abbasid Palace. Kadhimiya golden-domed mosque. Al-Rasheed Street evening walk.

  3. 3

    Babylon — Ancient Wonder

    Drive south to Babylon (90km). Full day at the ancient ruins: the Ishtar Gate reconstruction, Processional Way, Palace of Nebuchadnezzar II, site of the Hanging Gardens, and the Babylon Museum. Return to Baghdad for evening.

  4. 4

    Samarra & Departure

    Morning drive north to Samarra. Visit the iconic Malwiya spiral minaret of the Great Mosque of Samarra. Return to Baghdad for afternoon flight.
