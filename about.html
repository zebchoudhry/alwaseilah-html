About Alwaseilah Tours

Founded in 2009 by Adam Jebrin — an Iraqi-born guide who believes his country is one of the most important places on earth to visit.

Adam Jebrin

Founder & Lead Guide

Born in Iraq. Based in the UK. Running tours to Iraq since 2009.

Why I started this

I left Iraq as a young man and settled in the UK. Like many Iraqis in the diaspora, I watched from a distance as my country went through extraordinary upheaval — the 2003 war, the occupation, the insurgency, and eventually the partial stabilisation that followed. Through all of it, I stayed connected to Iraq. I visited regularly and I knew something that the news coverage rarely showed: that beneath the violence, Iraq is one of the most historically significant, culturally rich and genuinely hospitable places on earth.

In 2009, I started taking small groups of international travellers to Iraq. I wanted to show people the country I knew — not the country they had seen on the news. The response was immediate and overwhelming. Every group came back changed. Not because Iraq is easy. But because it is real, ancient, and alive in a way that few destinations can match.

What makes our tours different

The most important difference is local knowledge that cannot be bought. I know Iraq. I know which roads are fine and which to avoid. I know the sites that require advance permits and how to obtain them. I know the communities along our routes and the contacts we have built over 17 years. When something unexpected happens — a checkpoint, a schedule change, a site that is temporarily closed — we adapt without drama because we have been doing this long enough to have seen everything.

The second difference is honesty. I do not tell people that Iraq is safe in order to sell a tour. I tell people exactly what the situation is in the regions we visit, and I will cancel or postpone a tour if conditions are not right. Our reputation depends entirely on bringing people home safely with an experience they will talk about for the rest of their lives.

The third difference is depth. I am not a professional entertainer who delivers the same script to every group. I am Iraqi. This is my country. When I take you to the Ziggurat of Ur or the Shrine of Imam Hussain, I am not just reciting facts — I am showing you something that matters to me personally. That comes through in every visit.

Our company

Alwaseilah Tours is based in the UK and operates in Iraq. All tours are run by Adam personally or by guides he has trained and trusts.

We have been reviewed on TripAdvisor and TourHQ. We work with a small number of trusted local partners in Iraq for accommodation and transport. We do not use large coach operators or mass-market hotel chains — we use the people and places that give our tours their character.
