Iraq & Iran Pilgrimage
Najaf to Mashhad

🕒 14 days👥 Max 12🌍 Iraq + Iran🛂 Both visas included

The complete Shia pilgrimage journey combining Iraq's holiest cities with Iran's most sacred shrines. Fourteen days from Najaf to Mashhad — two countries, two visa processes, one seamless itinerary managed entirely by Alwaseilah Tours.

Begin at the shrine of Imam Ali in Najaf and the shrines of Karbala. Cross into Iran and travel through Tehran and Qom before reaching Mashhad and the magnificent shrine of Imam Reza — one of the largest mosque complexes in the world. This combined tour is the most spiritually complete pilgrimage available to English-speaking travellers and is planned meticulously to avoid logistical headaches.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Najaf Airport, Iraq

    Meet at Najaf International Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening orientation.

  2. 2

    Najaf — Shrine of Imam Ali

    Full day at the Imam Ali Shrine and Wadi al-Salam. Personal ziyarat time in the evening.

  3. 3

    Kufa & Najaf deeper visits

    Morning trip to Kufa Mosque. Afternoon at Al-Sahlah Mosque and Hannana Mosque.

  4. 4

    Travel to Karbala

    Move to Karbala. First visit to Imam Hussain Shrine and Abbas ibn Ali Shrine. Evening along Bain al-Haramayn.

  5. 5

    Karbala — full pilgrimage day

    Dedicated day for ziyarat of Imam Hussain and Abbas. Karbala Museum. Visit shrines of the companions.

  6. 6

    Baghdad — transit to Iran

    Drive to Baghdad. Afternoon flight to Tehran. Hotel check-in. Brief orientation.

  7. 7

    Tehran — shrines and Grand Bazaar

    Shah Abdol-Azim Shrine in Rey district, one of Iran's major pilgrimage sites. Tehran Grand Bazaar. Imam Khomeini Mausoleum.

  8. 8

    Qom — City of Scholars

    Drive to Qom (140km south). Full day at the Shrine of Fatima al-Masumeh (sister of Imam Reza). Qom's historic mosques and theological seminaries.

  9. 9

    Qom — deeper exploration

    Morning visits to Jamkaran Mosque and further sites in Qom. Afternoon departure to Isfahan or return to Tehran.

  10. 10

    Flight to Mashhad

    Internal flight to Mashhad in northeastern Iran. Transfer to hotel near the shrine complex.

  11. 11

    Mashhad — Shrine of Imam Reza

    Full day at the Imam Reza Shrine complex — the largest mosque complex in the world by area. Guided visit to the shrine, Goharshad Mosque, and the museum of the shrine.

  12. 12

    Mashhad — continued ziyarat

    Second full day at the shrine complex for personal prayer and deeper exploration. Visit the old bazaar of Mashhad.

  13. 13

    Free day in Mashhad

    Unstructured final full day for personal reflection, last visits and shopping. Farewell group dinner.

  14. 14

    Departure — Mashhad Airport

    Transfer to Mashhad International Airport for return flights.
