The Grand Tour

🕒 16 days👥 Max 10🌍 South to North Iraq🏆 The definitive Iraq journey

The most complete Iraq journey available to English-speaking travellers. Sixteen days from Basra on the Persian Gulf to Erbil in the Kurdistan mountains — every major historical site, every holy city, every landscape that makes Iraq one of the most extraordinary destinations on earth.

This is the tour for travellers who understand that Iraq is not a destination to rush. You will see everything: the Mesopotamian Marshes, ancient Ur, Babylon, the holy cities of Najaf and Karbala, Baghdad, Samarra, Mosul, Nimrud, the Yazidi valley, and the mountain towns of Kurdistan. Group size is capped at 10 to make the logistics manageable. This tour runs twice a year.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Basra Airport

    Fly into Basra International Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening introduction walk along the Shatt al-Arab waterway.

  2. 2

    Basra — port city of the Gulf

    Old Basra waterways, Sinbad Island, Corniche, Basra Museum, Martyr's Monument. Basra's historic role as a Persian Gulf trading city.

  3. 3

    Mesopotamian Marshes

    Boat trip through the UNESCO-listed Mesopotamian Marshes. Visit a Marsh Arab village. The landscape that inspired the biblical Garden of Eden.

  4. 4

    Ur — Ziggurat of the Moon God

    Ancient city of Ur, birthplace of Abraham. The Ziggurat of Ur (2100 BC). Royal Tombs of Ur. Drive north to Najaf.

  5. 5

    Najaf & Kufa

    Shrine of Imam Ali. Wadi al-Salam. Kufa Mosque. Al-Sahlah Mosque. Overnight Najaf.

  6. 6

    Karbala

    Shrine of Imam Hussain and Abbas ibn Ali. Bain al-Haramayn. Karbala Museum. Overnight Karbala.

  7. 7

    Babylon

    Full day at ancient Babylon: Ishtar Gate, Processional Way, Nebuchadnezzar's Palace, Babylon Museum. Drive north to Baghdad.

  8. 8

    Baghdad — museums and capital

    Iraq National Museum. Abbasid Palace. Mutanabbi Street. Kadhimiya Mosque. Tigris Corniche evening.

  9. 9

    Samarra spiral minaret

    Day trip to Samarra. Malwiya spiral minaret. Al-Askari Shrine. Return to Baghdad.

  10. 10

    Drive north — Tikrit and Assyrian sites

    Drive north along the Tigris. Stop at historic sites en route. Overnight Mosul area.

  11. 11

    Mosul — ancient Nineveh and rebuilding

    Ancient Nineveh ruins. Mosul old city restoration. Al-Nouri Mosque and leaning minaret. Overnight Mosul.

  12. 12

    Nimrud — Assyrian capital

    Ancient city of Nimrud (9th century BC). Winged bull statues. Palace ruins. Drive to Erbil.

  13. 13

    Erbil — world's oldest city

    Erbil Citadel (UNESCO). Erbil Civilisation Museum. Historic bazaars. Mudhafaria Minaret.

  14. 14

    Lalish — Yazidi sacred valley

    Visit Lalish, the holiest Yazidi site. Ancient temples and shrines. One of the world's oldest living religious traditions.

  15. 15

    Amadiyah — mountain town

    Drive up to Amadiyah, the medieval mountain fortress town. Spectacular views. Duhok valley. Kurdistan highlands at their finest.

  16. 16

    Return to Erbil & Departure

    Return to Erbil. Morning at leisure. Transfer to Erbil International Airport. Farewell.
