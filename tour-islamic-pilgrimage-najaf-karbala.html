Islamic Pilgrimage
Najaf, Karbala & Kufa

🕒 10 days 👥 Max 12 🌍 Iraq 🗣️ English-speaking guide

Walk in the footsteps of the Imams across Iraq's three holiest cities. This ten-day pilgrimage takes you to the shrines of Imam Ali in Najaf, Imam Hussain and Imam Abbas in Karbala, and the Mosque of Imam Ali in Kufa — sites of profound spiritual significance for Muslims across the world.

Led by Adam Jebrin, an Iraqi-born guide who has made this journey dozens of times and understands its religious depth, every visit is given the space and reverence it deserves. Groups are kept to a maximum of 12 so you can move at the pace of pilgrimage rather than tourism.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Baghdad (Najaf Airport)

    Fly into Najaf International Airport. Airport transfer to your hotel in Najaf. Evening welcome briefing, orientation walk around the old city and the illuminated dome of the Imam Ali Shrine.

  2. 2

    Najaf — Shrine of Imam Ali

    Full day at the Shrine of Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib, one of the holiest sites in Islam. Guided visit includes Wadi al-Salam cemetery, the world's largest. Afternoon at leisure for personal prayer and reflection.

  3. 3

    Kufa — Mosque of Imam Ali & Kufa Mosque

    Day trip to Kufa (30 minutes from Najaf). Visit the historic Kufa Mosque where Imam Ali led prayers, the House of Muslim ibn Aqeel, and the site of the Mihrab. Return to Najaf for evening.

  4. 4

    Najaf — Al-Sahlah Mosque & deeper exploration

    Morning visit to Al-Sahlah Mosque and Hannana Mosque. Afternoon free for personal ziyarat and shopping in the bazaar near the shrine. Evening group dinner.

  5. 5

    Travel to Karbala

    Morning departure to Karbala (80km north). Check in and first visit to the Shrine of Imam Hussain ibn Ali and the Shrine of Abbas ibn Ali. Evening walk along the Bain al-Haramayn, the sacred road between the two shrines.

  6. 6

    Karbala — Full pilgrimage day

    Dedicated full day for Ziyarat of Imam Hussain and Imam Abbas. Visit Karbala Museum and the site of the Battle of Karbala (Ashura plain). Evening lecture on the significance of Karbala in Islamic history.

  7. 7

    Karbala — Ziyarat of Companions

    Morning visits to the shrines of companions and martyrs of Karbala including Habib ibn Madhahir and Hurr ibn Yazid. Afternoon at leisure for personal prayer. Optional bazaar visit.

  8. 8

    Return to Najaf — Wadi al-Salam

    Return to Najaf for final visits and deeper exploration of Wadi al-Salam, the world's oldest and largest cemetery. Evening visit to the illuminated Shrine of Imam Ali after dark.

  9. 9

    Free day — personal reflection and final ziyarat

    Unstructured day for personal pilgrimage, last visits to the shrines and quiet reflection. Optional return visits to any site on the itinerary. Group farewell dinner in the evening.

  10. 10

    Departure — Najaf Airport

    Transfer to Najaf International Airport for return flights. The guide accompanies the group to the airport.
