Cradle of Civilisation
Historical Iraq

🕒 9 days 👥 Max 12 🌍 Iraq 🏛️ UNESCO World Heritage Sites

Iraq is the original cradle of human civilisation — home to Sumer, Babylon, and Assyria. This nine-day historical tour traces humanity's oldest story from the ancient ruins of Babylon to the Mesopotamian Marshes, where the world's first cities grew between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers five thousand years ago.

Most international tourists have never visited these sites. You will walk through Babylon's Ishtar Gate, stand at the base of the Ziggurat of Ur, and glide through the reed marshes of southern Iraq — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the landscape described in the Epic of Gilgamesh. Adam Jebrin provides the historical and cultural context that turns ruins into stories.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Baghdad International Airport

    Meet and greet at Baghdad airport. Transfer to hotel in central Baghdad. Evening orientation walk along the Tigris Corniche. Briefing on the tour programme.

  2. 2

    Baghdad — Capital of the Abbasid Caliphate

    Full day in Baghdad: the National Museum of Iraq (world's most significant collection of Mesopotamian artefacts), Mutanabbi Street book market, Abbasid Palace, Kadhimiya golden-domed mosque, and the Baghdad Zoo ruins.

  3. 3

    Samarra — The Spiral Minaret

    Day trip north to Samarra (120km). Visit the iconic Malwiya spiral minaret of the Great Mosque of Samarra — one of the most distinctive structures in the Islamic world. Also the Al-Askari Shrine with its famous golden dome.

  4. 4

    Babylon — The Ancient Wonder

    Drive south to Babylon (90km from Baghdad). Full day exploring the ancient city: the Ishtar Gate reconstruction, the Palace of Nebuchadnezzar II, the Processional Way, and the site of the legendary Hanging Gardens. Visit the Babylon Museum.

  5. 5

    Najaf & the journey south

    Continue south to Najaf. Brief visit to Wadi al-Salam cemetery and the old city of Najaf. Overnight in Najaf before continuing to the deep south.

  6. 6

    Ur — Ziggurat of the Moon God Nanna

    Drive to Nasiriyah. Visit the ancient city of Ur — birthplace of Abraham and centre of Sumerian civilisation. Climb the remarkably preserved Ziggurat of Ur (2100 BC), one of the best-preserved ancient structures on earth. Visit the Royal Tombs of Ur.

  7. 7

    Mesopotamian Marshes — the Garden of Eden

    Traditional boat trip through the Mesopotamian Marshes — the largest wetland ecosystem in western Eurasia and the model for the biblical Garden of Eden. Visit a Marsh Arab (Ma'dan) village and see how people have lived in these reeds for 5,000 years.

  8. 8

    Basra — Port City of the Persian Gulf

    Drive to Basra. Visit the Old Basra waterways and floating markets, Sinbad Island, the Corniche, and the Martyrs' Monument. Basra was the setting for some of the Arabian Nights stories. Overnight in Basra.

  9. 9

    Departure — Basra Airport

    Transfer to Basra International Airport for return flights. Optional morning visit to Basra Museum before departure.
