Booking Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms before making a booking enquiry.

Last updated: July 2026. These terms apply to all bookings with Alwaseilah Tours Ltd.

1. Booking and deposit

To confirm a place on any tour, a non-refundable deposit of 20% of the total tour price is required. Your booking is confirmed when Alwaseilah Tours receives the deposit and issues a written confirmation. The balance is due 60 days before the tour departure date. For bookings made within 60 days of departure, the full tour price is due at the time of booking.

2. Cancellation by the traveller

If you cancel a confirmed booking, the following cancellation charges apply:

  • More than 90 days before departure: deposit forfeited
  • 60–90 days before departure: 50% of total tour price
  • 30–59 days before departure: 75% of total tour price
  • Less than 30 days before departure: 100% of total tour price

All cancellations must be made in writing by email to info@alwaseilahtours.com. The date of cancellation is the date on which Alwaseilah Tours receives the written notice.

3. Cancellation or change by Alwaseilah Tours

We reserve the right to cancel or modify any tour due to safety concerns, minimum group numbers not being reached, Force Majeure events (including war, civil unrest, natural disaster, or travel restrictions), or any other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

If we cancel a tour: we will offer you a full refund of all monies paid, or the option to transfer to another departure.

If we make a significant change (route, dates, or price increase greater than 10%): we will notify you as soon as possible and offer you the option to accept the change or receive a full refund.

4. What is included

Unless stated otherwise in the tour description, the following are included in the tour price:

  • All accommodation as stated in the itinerary
  • All transport within the tour itinerary
  • English-speaking guide for the duration of the tour
  • Iraq visa processing assistance (visa fee included)
  • Entrance fees to all sites listed in the itinerary

The following are not included unless specifically stated: international flights, travel insurance, meals not specified in the itinerary, personal expenses, tips and gratuities.

5. Travel insurance

Travel insurance is mandatory for all participants. You must hold a valid policy that covers medical expenses, evacuation, cancellation, and travel to Iraq for the duration of your tour. You may be asked to provide proof of insurance before departure.

6. Health and fitness

All participants are responsible for assessing their own health and fitness for the tour. Some tours involve walking on uneven ground, extreme temperatures, and limited medical facilities. If you have a pre-existing medical condition that may affect your participation, please disclose this at the time of enquiry. We reserve the right to decline a booking on health or safety grounds.

7. Responsibility and liability

Alwaseilah Tours acts as a tour operator and accepts responsibility for the proper performance of the services described in the tour itinerary. We do not accept liability for events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of war, terrorism, natural disaster, government action, or the independent actions of third-party service providers.

Our liability for any claim is limited to the cost of the tour. We are not liable for indirect losses including loss of earnings or consequential loss.

8. Behaviour and conduct

All participants are expected to behave respectfully at all religious and cultural sites. Appropriate dress must be worn at mosques and shrines. Alwaseilah Tours reserves the right to remove any participant from a tour if their behaviour is deemed unsafe, disrespectful, or disruptive to the group, without refund.

9. Changes to terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version in force at the time of your booking confirmation applies to your tour.

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
