Is Iraq Safe to Visit?

An honest answer from people who have been running tours to Iraq since 2009.

We will always give you the honest answer. We do not downplay risks to sell a tour. Every enquirer gets a frank assessment of the current situation before they book. If conditions are not right, we will tell you.

The honest picture in 2026

Iraq is not one place. It is a country the size of California with very different security situations depending on the region. The Iraq you hear about in the news is often not the Iraq our tour groups visit.

Southern Iraq — Najaf, Karbala, Basra — has been stable for years and receives hundreds of thousands of Shia pilgrims annually from Iran, Pakistan and beyond. Our Islamic Pilgrimage Tour and South Iraq Express operate here every year. Iraqi Kurdistan is as safe as many European destinations and has its own tourism infrastructure — see our North Iraq & Kurdistan Tour and 16-day Grand Tour. Baghdad is a functioning capital city where millions of people live ordinary lives.

There are parts of Iraq we do not visit and will not visit. The Syrian border areas, Anbar province, and areas of Diyala remain dangerous. We have never operated in those regions.

What the FCO says

The UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) advises against all but essential travel to most of Iraq. This is a blanket advisory that covers the entire country for insurance and liability reasons. It does not mean that every part of Iraq is equally dangerous.

Iraqi Kurdistan has separate FCDO guidance that is less restrictive. We discuss the precise FCDO status for every region on every tour during your pre-booking call.

You should always check the latest FCDO guidance at gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/iraq.

Seventeen years of tours

Alwaseilah Tours has been operating since 2009. We have run tours through the end of the US occupation, the rise and fall of ISIS, and the subsequent stabilisation of the regions we operate in. We have never had a security incident that put a group member in danger.

This is not luck. It is careful route planning, local intelligence, relationships with security forces and communities, and an absolute commitment to not operating when conditions are not right. In some years we have cancelled or postponed tours. We will always do so rather than take unnecessary risks.

Travel insurance

Specialist travel insurance is essential for Iraq. Standard travel insurance policies do not cover Iraq. You need a specialist policy that explicitly covers the regions on your itinerary. We can recommend providers who offer this when you enquire.

Who visits Iraq with us

Our groups include UK, US, Canadian and Australian travellers. Journalists, academics, pilgrims, historians, photographers and curious travellers who have exhausted more conventional destinations. The question we hear most often after a tour: "Why did I wait so long to come here?"

Your questions answered

Do I need a visa for Iraq from the UK?

Yes. UK citizens require a visa for Iraq. We handle the visa process for every tour — it is included in our price. See our Iraq eVisa guide 2026 for full details.

Is there a dress code?

Modest dress is expected throughout Iraq. Women should cover their hair and wear loose, full-length clothing at religious sites. Men should wear long trousers at all sites. We provide a full pre-tour packing guide.

What medical precautions do I need?

We recommend consulting a travel doctor before departure. Iraq has high summer temperatures (45°C+). October to April are the best months to travel. We carry a first aid kit on every tour and brief the group on hydration and heat management.

Can I travel independently in Iraq?

Technically yes, but we would not recommend it for first-time visitors. Access to many sites requires permits arranged in advance, and language is a significant barrier. The value of a guide who knows the country is substantial.

What is the best time of year to visit?

October to April. Iraqi summers are extreme — June to August temperatures regularly exceed 45°C in Baghdad and the south. Spring (March–April) offers the best combination of temperature and green landscape.
