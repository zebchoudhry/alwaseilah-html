Iraq eVisa Guide 2026

Everything UK, US, Australian and European travellers need to know about getting into Iraq.

Visa included in all tours. Alwaseilah Tours handles full visa processing assistance for every booking. We guide you through the eVisa application, check your documents, and confirm everything is in order before submission.

Do you need a visa?

Yes. UK, US, Canadian, Australian and most European nationals require a visa to enter Iraq. There is no visa-on-arrival for most Western nationalities at Baghdad, Najaf or Basra airports, though this changes — always confirm with us before travel.

Iraqi Kurdistan (Erbil, Sulaymaniyah airports) has historically operated a simpler visa system for some nationalities. We will advise you on the correct process for your nationality and your tour entry point when you book.

The Iraq eVisa system

Iraq operates an online eVisa system for tourist and pilgrimage visas. The application is submitted online, and the approved visa is delivered electronically. You print it and present it at the border.

Key details for 2026:

  • Cost: Approximately $75–$100 USD (varies by nationality)
  • Processing time: 5–10 business days from application
  • Validity: Single entry, 30 days
  • Extension: Possible in-country at immigration offices (we assist with this on longer tours)

What documents you need

For the standard tourist eVisa application you will need:

  1. A valid passport with at least 6 months remaining validity and two blank pages
  2. A digital passport photo (white background, recent)
  3. Your tour booking confirmation or invitation letter (we provide this)
  4. Hotel confirmation for your first night (we provide this)
  5. A return flight itinerary
  6. Travel insurance documents (required)
  7. Payment card (credit or debit) for the visa fee

Israeli passport stamp

Iraq does not admit travellers with Israeli passport stamps. If your passport contains Israeli stamps, you will need a new passport before applying for an Iraq visa. Some travellers ask Israeli border control to stamp a separate piece of paper rather than the passport — this is worth planning ahead if you travel to Israel regularly.

What happens at the airport

On arrival at Najaf, Baghdad, Basra or Erbil airports, you will present your eVisa print-out, your passport and your biometrics will be taken. This is a standard immigration process. Our guide or a local contact meets you airside or at arrivals and assists if there are any questions from immigration officers.

The process typically takes 15–30 minutes at Najaf and Erbil. Baghdad International can be slower. We factor border processing time into all tour pickup schedules.

Iraq–Iran combined visa

If you are booking the Iraq–Iran Pilgrimage tour, you need separate visas for both countries. Iran has its own eVisa system. We handle both applications as part of the tour booking process. Iranian visas for UK and US nationals require additional processing — allow 4 weeks minimum.

Common mistakes to avoid

Applying too early

The 30-day visa validity begins from the date of issue, not your travel date. Apply no more than 3 weeks before departure. We time this for you.

Wrong entry point on the application

Your eVisa is issued for a specific port of entry. If you change your entry airport (e.g., from Baghdad to Najaf), you may need to reapply. Always confirm your entry point with us before applying.

Passport expiring within 6 months

Iraq requires your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your departure date. Check your passport expiry date before booking.

Not printing the eVisa

The eVisa is a physical document you must print and present at the border. A digital copy on your phone is not accepted. Print two copies.
