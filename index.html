Why Travel With Us

We have been running Iraq tours since 2009. That experience shows in every detail — from the route plan to the airport pickup.

Iraqi-born guide since 2009

Adam Jebrin was born in Iraq and has led groups across every region for over 17 years. He knows which roads are open, which sites need permits, and who to call when plans change.

Honest safety briefings

We do not hide the reality of travel in Iraq. Every enquirer gets a straight answer about current conditions, FCO guidance and our on-the-ground assessment before they book.

Small groups, personal service

Every tour is capped at 10–12 people. No crowds, no conveyor belt. You travel with people who share your interest, led by a guide who knows your name.

Visa support included

Iraq visas for UK, US and European travellers are included in every itinerary. We handle the paperwork, you handle the packing.

English-speaking guides

Every tour includes a guide who speaks fluent English and understands what English-speaking travellers need — context, translation and a frank answer to every question.

Full logistics, zero guesswork

Accommodation, transport, permits, entrance fees, airport transfers — all arranged. You arrive and follow the guide. Everything else is handled.
