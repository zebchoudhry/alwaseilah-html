Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Last updated: July 2026. This policy covers Alwaseilah Tours Ltd, Company No. 06860878, registered in England and Wales. We are the data controller for personal information you provide to us.

1. What information we collect

We collect personal information only when you actively provide it to us. This includes:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number
  • Booking information: nationality, passport details (required for visa processing), group size, travel dates
  • Enquiry details: any questions or preferences you share in your message

We do not use cookies for tracking, run advertising on this site, or collect any data automatically from visitors.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to tour enquiries
  • Process and manage bookings
  • Handle visa applications on your behalf (when you have booked a tour)
  • Contact you about your booking (changes, updates, pre-departure information)

We do not use your information for marketing without your consent. We will not send you promotional emails unless you specifically request them.

3. Who we share it with

We share your information only where necessary:

  • Iraqi visa authorities: passport and personal details required for visa processing
  • Accommodation providers: name and stay dates
  • Our contact form processor (Formspree): to receive enquiry submissions. Formspree operates under its own privacy policy

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for 12 months. Booking data (including financial records) is kept for 7 years as required by UK law. You can request deletion of enquiry data at any time if you have not proceeded to booking.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (where no legal obligation to retain it applies)
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@alwaseilahtours.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information. Our website uses HTTPS. Enquiry data submitted via the contact form is processed by Formspree and stored in our email system.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at:

Alwaseilah Tours Ltd
Email: info@alwaseilahtours.com
Phone: +44 7947 138673

If you are not satisfied with how we handle a complaint, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
