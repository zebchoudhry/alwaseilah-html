Historical Iraq
8-Day Classic

🕒 8 days👥 Max 12🌍 Iraq🏛️ Babylon to Samarra

Iraq's greatest historical and spiritual sites in one comprehensive eight-day classic. This is the best-value introduction to the full sweep of Iraqi history — from the ancient Sumerian city of Ur (3000 BC) to the Abbasid Caliphate's spiral minaret at Samarra (860 AD) — with Babylon, Karbala, Najaf and Baghdad in between.

This tour is the sweet spot for most travellers: long enough to visit everything meaningful, short enough to fit within a standard work holiday allocation. It is the most popular itinerary Alwaseilah Tours runs.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Baghdad International Airport

    Meet at Baghdad airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening walk along the Tigris. Briefing on the tour.

  2. 2

    Baghdad — National Museum & city tour

    Iraq National Museum. Abbasid Palace. Mutanabbi Street. Kadhimiya Mosque. Afternoon at leisure in central Baghdad.

  3. 3

    Samarra — spiral minaret

    Day trip north to Samarra. Malwiya spiral minaret. Al-Askari Shrine with its golden dome. Return to Baghdad.

  4. 4

    Babylon — ancient wonder

    Drive south to Babylon. Full day: Ishtar Gate, Processional Way, Nebuchadnezzar's Palace, Babylon Museum. Overnight south.

  5. 5

    Karbala — shrines and ziyarat

    Visit the Shrine of Imam Hussain and Shrine of Abbas ibn Ali. Bain al-Haramayn. Overnight Karbala or Najaf.

  6. 6

    Najaf — Imam Ali Shrine & Kufa

    Full day in Najaf: Shrine of Imam Ali, Wadi al-Salam, Kufa Mosque. Overnight Najaf.

  7. 7

    Ur — Ziggurat of Sumer

    Drive to Nasiriyah. Visit the ancient city of Ur and the Ziggurat of Ur (2100 BC). Optional afternoon at the Mesopotamian Marshes. Return north.

  8. 8

    Return to Baghdad & Departure

    Drive back to Baghdad. Optional morning visit to any site missed. Transfer to Baghdad International Airport.
