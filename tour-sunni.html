Sunni Heritage Tour
Baghdad, Samarra & Mosul

🕒 7 days👥 Max 12🌍 Baghdad · Samarra · Mosul🕌 Abbasid & Sunni heritage

A seven-day journey through the great Sunni and Abbasid heritage of Iraq — from the shrines of Imam Abu Hanifa and Sheikh Abdul Qadir al-Gilani in Baghdad, to the towering Malwiya minaret and Great Mosque of Samarra, and on to the rebuilding old city of Mosul.

Iraq is the heartland of early Islamic civilisation. This tour focuses on the sites most meaningful to Sunni visitors: the resting places of two of the most influential figures in Islamic scholarship, the imperial mosques of the Abbasid Caliphate, and the historic quarters of Baghdad and Mosul. Every day is guided by someone who knows the history, the etiquette and the story behind each site.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Baghdad

    Fly into Baghdad International Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit to Adhamiyah and the Mosque and shrine of Imam Abu Hanifa al-Nu'man, founder of the Hanafi school of jurisprudence.

  2. 2

    Baghdad — Sunni heritage of the old city

    Visit the Mosque and shrine of Sheikh Abdul Qadir al-Gilani (al-Gaylani). Explore the Mustansiriya Madrasa — one of the world's oldest universities (13th century) — the Abbasid Palace, and historic Mutanabbi Street.

  3. 3

    Samarra — Great Mosque & the Malwiya

    Day trip north to Samarra, the Abbasid imperial capital. Climb the iconic Malwiya spiral minaret at the Great Mosque of Samarra, once the largest mosque in the world, and visit the Abu Dulaf Mosque and the ruins of the caliphal city.

  4. 4

    Baghdad to Mosul

    Drive north to Mosul via Tikrit, following the Tigris. Check in to your Mosul hotel and take an evening orientation walk along the riverfront.

  5. 5

    Mosul — old city and the Great Mosque of al-Nuri

    Full day in Mosul: the Great Mosque of al-Nuri and its famous al-Hadba (leaning) minaret, currently being rebuilt, the restored old city and markets, and the shrine of Nabi Yunus (Prophet Jonah).

  6. 6

    Nineveh & the ancient north

    Visit ancient Nineveh, the Assyrian capital, and the surrounding heritage sites. Afternoon return south, or transfer to Erbil for onward flights.

  7. 7

    Departure

    Morning at leisure. Transfer to the airport for your departure flight.
