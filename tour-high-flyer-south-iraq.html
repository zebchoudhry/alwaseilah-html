High Flyer
South Iraq Express

🕒 4 days👥 Max 10🌍 Iraq⚡ Fast-paced highlights

Iraq's most iconic sights condensed into four days — built for travellers with limited time who refuse to compromise on the highlights. Basra's port city atmosphere, Najaf's holiest shrine, Karbala's sacred sites, ancient Babylon, and Samarra's extraordinary spiral minaret.

The High Flyer tours are fast-paced by design. You will cover significant ground each day. This is the right tour if you want a first taste of Iraq before committing to a longer journey — or if your schedule simply does not allow for more time. Maximum group size is 10 to keep logistics tight.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 1

    Arrival — Basra Airport

    Fly into Basra. Transfer to hotel. Afternoon visit to Basra waterways and Corniche. Evening briefing and group dinner.

  2. 2

    Najaf & Karbala

    Early start north to Najaf. Visit Imam Ali Shrine and Wadi al-Salam. Continue to Karbala for visits to the Imam Hussain and Abbas shrines. Overnight in Karbala or Najaf.

  3. 3

    Babylon & Baghdad

    Drive north to ancient Babylon. Full visit to the ruins, Ishtar Gate and Nebuchadnezzar's Palace. Continue to Baghdad. Evening on the Tigris Corniche.

  4. 4

    Samarra & Departure

    Morning drive to Samarra spiral minaret. Return to Baghdad for afternoon departure. Transfer to Baghdad International Airport.
